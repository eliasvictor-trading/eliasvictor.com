About

Elias Victor, LLC is a California-based trading technology company focused on building structured tools for futures traders.

The work emphasizes opening range behavior, multi-timeframe context, VWAP, moving averages, alert logic, and disciplined execution frameworks.

Tools are developed in Pine Script v6 and refined against live market conditions before publication.
