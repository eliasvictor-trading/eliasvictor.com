About

Elias Victor, LLC is a California-based trading technology company focused on building structured TradingView tools for CME futures traders.

The work emphasizes opening range behavior, multi-timeframe context, VWAP, moving averages, alert logic, and disciplined execution frameworks.

Tools are developed in Pine Script v6 and refined against live market conditions before publication.

Selected repositories may be published with source code under their own license terms. Site copy, branding, and other proprietary materials remain the property of Elias Victor, LLC. All tools are provided for educational purposes only.
